# Primary sources

Verify anything load-bearing here before you file. Federal rules are stable;
state fees and thresholds are not.

## IRS

- Charities and Nonprofits hub — https://www.irs.gov/charities-and-nonprofits
- Publication 557, *Tax-Exempt Status for Your Organization* —
  https://www.irs.gov/publications/p557
- Form 1023 and instructions — https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023
- Form 1023-EZ eligibility worksheet — https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023-ez
- Form 1024 (c6 and others) — https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1024
- Form 5768, § 501(h) lobbying election — https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-5768
- Lobbying and political campaign activity guidance —
  https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/lobbying
- Unrelated business income tax — https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/unrelated-business-income-tax
- Public charity vs private foundation status —
  https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/public-charities
- Tax Exempt Organization Search (look up peers' 990s) —
  https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/

## Statutes

- 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3), § 501(c)(6), § 501(h), § 170, § 162(e), § 512–514 (UBIT),
  § 527(f), § 4958, § 6033(e), § 1202 (QSBS) — https://uscode.house.gov/

## State

- Secretary of State for your chosen state — formation forms, fees, annual reports.
- Attorney General charities bureau — registration, annual filings, audit
  thresholds. New York: https://www.charitiesnys.com/ · California:
  https://oag.ca.gov/charities
- Delaware Division of Corporations — https://corp.delaware.gov/
- Multistate charitable solicitation overview —
  https://www.multistatefiling.org/

## Useful secondary sources

- Council of Nonprofits, state-by-state guides —
  https://www.councilofnonprofits.org/
- Bolder Advocacy (Alliance for Justice), on lobbying limits and c3/c4 pairs —
  https://bolderadvocacy.org/
- National Council of Nonprofits, fiscal sponsorship primers.
- Public Counsel and similar legal-aid nonprofit formation guides.
